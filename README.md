# LexSphere ⚖️

**LexSphere** is an AI-powered legal research prototype engineered to enhance legal discovery, statutory interpretation, case law analysis, and automated citation verification.

---

## 📌 Project Overview
LexSphere provides legal professionals and researchers with an intelligent platform that combines advanced Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) with strict citation validation and document analysis pipelines.

### Core Focus Areas
- **Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG):** Context-aware querying over legal statutes, precedents, and regulatory frameworks.
- **Document Processing & Citation Verification:** Parsing of complex legal briefs, extraction of authorities, and verification of cited sources to prevent hallucinations.
- **Modern User Interface:** An intuitive, responsive legal research workspace built with React and Vite.
